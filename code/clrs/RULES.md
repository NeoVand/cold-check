# The fitted rules, in words

Generated by `rules.py` from `results/<task>.json` (the final runs). A rule is the conjunction of its conditions; where it was not exact on the training answers, the sparse score that decides among the candidates it lets through is listed too. Pair rules: u is the candidate, v the target; item rules: i; k is any third item (not u, not v / not i). "not yet" values compare false with each other.
